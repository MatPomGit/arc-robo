\section{Introduction}
A sensor fusion model may require explicit failure detection and selective prediction to remain dependable under missing or corrupted modalities. The scientific contribution under consideration is a falsifiable comparison in which alternative explanations, measurement limitations and distinct hypotheses from related ARC Robo studies can be assessed independently. Both the source of observations and the assumptions needed to interpret results must be documented before any confirmatory comparison.

\section{Research question and hypothesis}
Does failure-aware selection lower worst-condition missed detections while respecting fixed false-positive and minimum-coverage budgets? The working hypothesis is that the proposed approach improves the primary endpoint under an explicitly matched evidence and resource budget. This hypothesis is subject to prior-art review and prospective specification; non-superiority and negative findings must remain reportable.

\section{Materials and methods}
Inject documented dropout, stale frames, blur and contrast degradation into independently labelled paired sequences; distinguish synthetic corruption from observed sensor faults. The experimental unit, independent ground truth, data inclusion criteria, version of software and sensors, and provenance of each dataset or scenario will be fixed in the protocol. Training, calibration and evaluation partitions will be isolated at the appropriate physical scene, sequence, participant or device level.

\subsection{Comparators and experimental controls}
Compare single-modality models, fixed fusion, quality-weighted fusion and failure-aware abstention on identical scene holdouts. Model or policy hyperparameters must not be selected from final held-out results. The implementation will record relevant software versions, seeds, sensor configuration, timestamps and data rights.

\subsection{Primary endpoint}
Worst-stratum miss rate at a locked false-positive-per-frame ceiling and minimum prediction coverage. Denominators, operational thresholds, missing observations and failure cases will be defined before inferential analysis. Secondary metrics will not be substituted post hoc for the primary endpoint.

\section{Statistical and computational analysis plan}
Estimate risk-coverage curves and worst-case miss-rate uncertainty by independent sequence and fault type. Any confirmatory statistical contrast will report uncertainty intervals, effective independent sample size and relevant failure strata. Where a physical validation or participant-based experiment is necessary, approval and feasibility assessment must precede data collection.

\section{Anticipated interpretation and limitations}
Average mAP improvements do not establish resilience under failure. The primary outcome must not duplicate the normal-condition fusion claim of RGBT-P02. Confounding from software versions, sensor conditions, scene selection and methodological overlap will be assessed explicitly. Claims about simulated behavior will not be generalized to physical safety or human behavior without matching evidence.

\section{Results}
Empirical results, tables and uncertainty intervals will be incorporated after execution of the documented evaluation protocol and independent verification of the input evidence.

\section{Discussion}
The planned comparison is intended to determine whether the proposed mechanism yields an identifiable gain over the stated controls. Interpretation must distinguish failure of a scientific hypothesis from an incomplete implementation, and should describe practical operating conditions under which conclusions remain valid.

\section{Conclusion}
This study defines a testable research problem, matched comparators and a primary evaluation target. A substantive empirical conclusion remains conditional on reproducible measurements and appropriate methodological review.
