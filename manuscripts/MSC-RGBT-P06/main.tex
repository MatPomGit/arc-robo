\begin{abstract}
Tracking requires temporal association and independently annotated identities, not frame-level detection accuracy alone. Does modality-aware temporal association reduce identity switches during occlusion and intermittent sensing compared with fixed-fusion and single-modality trackers? The prospective design is as follows: Use legally available paired RGB/LWIR video with verified timestamps, individual object IDs and sequence-disjoint training and test partitions. The primary outcome is Identity switches per annotated trajectory, with IDF1 and HOTA as secondary tracking metrics where annotations permit. The manuscript defines a research question and an evaluation strategy; it does not report experimental estimates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Tracking requires temporal association and independently annotated identities, not frame-level detection accuracy alone. The scientific contribution under consideration is a falsifiable comparison in which alternative explanations, measurement limitations and distinct hypotheses from related ARC Robo studies can be assessed independently. Both the source of observations and the assumptions needed to interpret results must be documented before any confirmatory comparison.

\section{Research question and hypothesis}
Does modality-aware temporal association reduce identity switches during occlusion and intermittent sensing compared with fixed-fusion and single-modality trackers? The working hypothesis is that the proposed approach improves the primary endpoint under an explicitly matched evidence and resource budget. This hypothesis is subject to prior-art review and prospective specification; non-superiority and negative findings must remain reportable.

\section{Materials and methods}
Use legally available paired RGB/LWIR video with verified timestamps, individual object IDs and sequence-disjoint training and test partitions. The experimental unit, independent ground truth, data inclusion criteria, version of software and sensors, and provenance of each dataset or scenario will be fixed in the protocol. Training, calibration and evaluation partitions will be isolated at the appropriate physical scene, sequence, participant or device level.

\subsection{Comparators and experimental controls}
Compare RGB tracking, LWIR tracking, fixed-fusion association and uncertainty-aware multimodal association with matched base detections. Model or policy hyperparameters must not be selected from final held-out results. The implementation will record relevant software versions, seeds, sensor configuration, timestamps and data rights.

\subsection{Primary endpoint}
Identity switches per annotated trajectory, with IDF1 and HOTA as secondary tracking metrics where annotations permit. Denominators, operational thresholds, missing observations and failure cases will be defined before inferential analysis. Secondary metrics will not be substituted post hoc for the primary endpoint.

\section{Statistical and computational analysis plan}
Report sequence-clustered identity-consistency estimates stratified by occlusion, missing frames and sensor availability. Any confirmatory statistical contrast will report uncertainty intervals, effective independent sample size and relevant failure strata. Where a physical validation or participant-based experiment is necessary, approval and feasibility assessment must precede data collection.

\section{Anticipated interpretation and limitations}
Still images, synthetic rectangle overlap and uncertain timestamp correspondence cannot substantiate real temporal tracking claims. Confounding from software versions, sensor conditions, scene selection and methodological overlap will be assessed explicitly. Claims about simulated behavior will not be generalized to physical safety or human behavior without matching evidence.

\section{Results}
Empirical results, tables and uncertainty intervals will be incorporated after execution of the documented evaluation protocol and independent verification of the input evidence.

\section{Discussion}
The planned comparison is intended to determine whether the proposed mechanism yields an identifiable gain over the stated controls. Interpretation must distinguish failure of a scientific hypothesis from an incomplete implementation, and should describe practical operating conditions under which conclusions remain valid.

\section{Conclusion}
This study defines a testable research problem, matched comparators and a primary evaluation target. A substantive empirical conclusion remains conditional on reproducible measurements and appropriate methodological review.
