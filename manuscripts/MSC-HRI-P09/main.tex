\begin{abstract}
Active scenario search could reveal safety-field counterexamples that uniform random and grid searches miss within the same evaluation budget. Does coverage-guided adversarial search discover independently verified severe violations more efficiently than budget-matched random, grid and conventional scenario searches? The prospective design is as follows: Execute simulation-only perturbations of crossing patterns, occlusion, payload geometry, stale telemetry and sensor dropout with fixed scenario seeds and independent scenario holdouts. The primary outcome is Number of distinct confirmed hazardous counterexamples per fixed simulator budget, together with time to first severe violation. The manuscript defines a research question and an evaluation strategy; it does not report experimental estimates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Active scenario search could reveal safety-field counterexamples that uniform random and grid searches miss within the same evaluation budget. The scientific contribution under consideration is a falsifiable comparison in which alternative explanations, measurement limitations and distinct hypotheses from related ARC Robo studies can be assessed independently. Both the source of observations and the assumptions needed to interpret results must be documented before any confirmatory comparison.

\section{Primary estimand and prospective decision rules}
Difference in unique independently confirmed severe counterexamples per fixed policy-evaluation budget across independent seed and scenario families. One primary falsification-efficiency test; H2/H3 treated as secondary unless familywise adjustment is preregistered. H1 is the primary scientific hypothesis unless the source protocol states a different frozen confirmatory contrast. H2 and H3 are supporting or exploratory until a prospective multiplicity strategy (e.g., Holm adjustment) is selected. Any sample size, quality boundary, non-inferiority margin, effect threshold or confidence limit not grounded in existing evidence is explicitly pending approval before confirmatory evaluation.

\section{Detailed scientific scope and novelty boundary}
This proposed methods paper evaluates efficiency of finding reproducible safety-field counterexamples, not predictive-field accuracy (MSC-HRI-P01), reachability calibration (P04), benchmark construction (P05) or protective fallback (P07). The evaluated controller and independent hazard oracle remain frozen while the search method changes.

\section{Falsifiable hypotheses}
\paragraph{H1.} Coverage- and uncertainty-guided adversarial scenario search uncovers more unique independently confirmed severe policy violations per fixed simulator-evaluation budget than random, stratified-grid or library-driven search.

\paragraph{H2.} The search finds its first confirmed severe counterexample in fewer evaluated scenarios than the strongest budget-matched baseline, accounting for runs in which no counterexample is found.

\paragraph{H3.} Counterexample families remain reproducible under fresh replay seeds and retain search value on held-out scenario families rather than only on the development simulator seed.

\section{Operationalization and study population}
\subsection{Independent unit of analysis}
One independent policy-scenario execution; framewise violations are collapsed into unique counterexamples using a frozen equivalence definition.

\subsection{Data, sensors and ground-truth requirements}
Pinned policy weights/code, simulator version, independent hazard oracle, bounded scenario generator, seed manifests, candidate rejections and unmodified replay traces. Data and models will be versioned with checksums, licensing information, instrument parameters, selection rules and clock provenance. The held-out test cohort must be independent at the acquisition-session, scene or participant level rather than at the individual frame level.

\subsection{Primary and secondary endpoints}
The primary estimand is defined as follows: Confirmed unique severe counterexamples per fixed count of simulator-policy evaluations; all invalid proposals and unsuccessful searches count toward the budget.

The secondary measurements will be: Evaluations until first severe violation, discovery coverage, severity distribution, proportion of independently replayable failures and compute time. The exact numerator, denominator, outcome threshold and permitted exclusions must be fixed in the protocol before inferential analysis.

\section{Planned experiments and research sequence}
\subsection{A. Operational hazard oracle}
Specify collision, separation and unsafe control as independently computed predicates with explicit geometric tolerance. Freeze feasible scene generation bounds, hazard severity and the equivalence rule for de-duplicating repeated violating frames.

\subsection{B. Equal-budget falsification benchmark}
Compare random sampling, grid/stratified search, a scenario library, active coverage optimization and the proposed uncertainty-guided falsifier under identical simulator-call budgets. Perturb multi-person crossings, occlusion, payload and delayed perception without changing controller weights.

\subsection{C. Held-out counterexample transfer}
Reserve scene layouts, trajectories and perturbation families outside search development. Independently replay discovered cases, verify oracle labels and inspect sensitivity to simulator parameters and numerical artefacts.

\subsection{D. Prior-art and ownership audit}
Review established risk-based test generation and adversarial falsification methods before promoting the candidate to a manuscript. If gains reproduce only ordinary parameter sweeps or duplicate M05/M07 outputs, fold the work into the parent studies.

\section{Comparative design and statistical inference}
Use paired independent-seed comparisons of discovery rate with confidence intervals. Use censored time-to-first-violation analysis for runs without a detected event. Validate generalization on held-out scenario families and report negative searches. When human participants or independent trajectories are needed, their number must follow a documented power simulation or precision calculation after a feasible pilot. Report uncertainty, negative results and sensitivity to failure strata. Thresholds, model configurations and exclusion criteria must be frozen before final testing.

\section{Evidence gates, reproducibility and ethical boundary}
Offline simulation and telemetry replay only. An adversarially found simulator failure is not physical humanoid safety evidence; require an independently implemented oracle and prior-art differentiation. Each study phase will preserve source SHA or release, configuration, labels, independent oracle, random seeds, raw event timing, and an auditable trace from inputs to computed figures. Simulated evidence is distinct from measured hardware outcomes. Participant studies require prior ethical review and consent.
